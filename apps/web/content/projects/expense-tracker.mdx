## Challenge
Users need a simple way to see where their money is going and make better financial decisions.

## Solution
I built a structured full-stack experience for logging financial activity, viewing balances, and tracking user spending behavior.

## What I Built
- Income and expense tracking flows
- Clear financial summaries for users
- A clean interface for adding and reviewing transactions
- A product structure that is easy to extend later
- Deployment-ready frontend and backend separation

## Impact
The app helps users make smarter decisions by giving them a clear picture of income and expenses.

## Why It Matters
It shows I can build practical products that are useful, not just technically interesting.
