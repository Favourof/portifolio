## Challenge
Users need a fast, installable way to track money and stay productive on mobile even when connectivity is unreliable.

## Solution
I built a structured full-stack experience for logging financial activity, viewing balances, tracking user spending patterns, and supporting a progressive web app workflow.

## What I Built
- Income and expense tracking flows
- Clear financial summaries for users
- A clean interface for adding and reviewing transactions
- A product structure that is easy to extend later
- Deployment-ready frontend and backend separation
- Progressive web app behavior for a more app-like experience

## Impact
The app helps users make smarter decisions by giving them a clear picture of income and expenses in a product they can use like a native app.

## Why It Matters
It shows I can build practical products that are useful, not just technically interesting.
